Filament cost calculator - Filament material cost calculator

Calculate the material cost of a print from its slicer weight and the net filament weight and price of a spool. Electricity, labor and machine wear are separate costs.

Quick answer: A $20, 1,000 g spool and a 50 g print cost $1.10 when 10% extra material is included.

How the calculation works

Divide spool price by net spool weight in grams to get cost per gram. Multiply by print weight and by one plus your extra-material percentage. Use the slicer total including supports and purge when available; only add an allowance for material that total excludes.

Worked examples

Example 1

A $20, 1,000 g spool and a 50 g print cost $1.10 when 10% extra material is included.

Example 2

A $30, 750 g spool and a 125 g slicer total cost $5.00 with no additional allowance.

What this estimate does not cover

Net spool weight means filament only, not the empty spool. The allowance is optional, not a recommended failure rate. Costs use the currency you select; the calculator does not convert exchange rates. This is material cost, not a selling price.
