Method and assumptions for filament cost calculator

Filament material cost calculator

Divide spool price by net spool weight in grams to get cost per gram. Multiply by print weight and by one plus your extra-material percentage. Use the slicer total including supports and purge when available; only add an allowance for material that total excludes.

Limits and interpretation

Net spool weight means filament only, not the empty spool. The allowance is optional, not a recommended failure rate. Costs use the currency you select; the calculator does not convert exchange rates. This is material cost, not a selling price.

Units and rounding

Lengths convert using exactly 0.3048 metres per foot and 2.54 centimetres per inch. Area and volume conversions use the corresponding squared and cubed factors. Counts round up where whole items must be purchased; wallpaper drops per roll and fabric pieces across a width round down. Displayed values may be rounded, while calculations retain additional precision.

Compare inputs with the worked examples on the calculator page. A different material, layout or measurement can require different assumptions.
