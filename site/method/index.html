Method and assumptions

Filament material cost calculator equation

Cost per gram = spool price / net spool weight; Material cost = cost per gram × print weight × (1 + extra / 100); Electricity cost = (printer watts / 1000) × print hours × rate per kWh.

Step-by-step logic

Divide spool price by net filament weight in grams to obtain unit cost per gram. Multiply by slicer material weight adjusted for any uncounted waste percentage. When electricity is enabled, multiply average wattage in kilowatts by print duration and your local energy tariff.

Units and rounding

Currency totals display to two decimal places while cost per gram displays to four decimal places so small prints remain readable. Calculations keep full floating-point precision internally.

Limits and edge cases

Net spool weight means plastic only—exclude the empty reel weight (often 180 g to 260 g). Changing the currency label does not convert exchange rates. Printer depreciation, failed jobs, post-processing, and labor are separate from material and power cost.
